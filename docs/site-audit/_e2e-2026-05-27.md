## Cross-site issues

1. **T2/T3 tier entries missing on 4 sites after bootstrap-to-maintain transition.** Caused by `transitionSiteToMaintain` not re-initializing `streamState` with the maintain-phase tier list `[2,3,4]` -- bootstrap only sets up `[4]`. Result: T2/T3 never run after transition because the scheduler skips tiers with no `tierState` entry. **FIXED during this audit:**
   - `maintain-readiness.ts`: transition now calls `ensureMaintainTiers(streamState)` to fill in missing T2/T3 entries (new helper in `stream-detector.ts`).
   - Retroactive fix applied to `corwin-arms.com`, `groupepronature.ca`, `tacord.com`, `tommyenterprises.com` via `_fix-missing-tiers-2026-05-27.ts`.
   - The 4 sites now show T2/T3 RED with reason `lastCycleCompletedAt unset` -- entries exist; scheduler hasn't cycled them yet. They will go GREEN once T2 (5 h cooldown) and T3 (9 h cooldown) complete a cycle.

2. **Live keyword search returns 0 matches** on `groupepronature.ca` (broad/accessory) and `tacord.com` (broad). For groupepronature this is likely WAF interference on the search endpoint (`hasWaf=true`). For tacord verify the adapter's `getSearchUrl` produces a working URL.

3. **Index gaps on broad keywords** for `aagcanada.ca/rifle` (60% live-in-FA) and `westernmetal.ca/rifle` (60% live-in-FA). 4 of 10 live page-1 results on aag and 2 of 5 on westernmetal are not in FA's ProductIndex -- could be very recent products not yet crawled, or category filters excluding them.
